Version 1.0, 26 August 2026

The AGPL-3.0-only software license does not grant permission to use these marks.

You may use the FoxLift name without written permission to:

- refer truthfully to the official, unmodified FoxLift software;
- state that another product works with FoxLift, without implying sponsorship or endorsement;
- discuss FoxLift in reviews, tutorials, news, and other commentary; or
- describe a fork as “based on FoxLift” when the fork has a distinct name and clearly states that
  it is independent and not endorsed by FoxLift or Timo Bejan.

Unmodified redistributions may retain the FoxLift name and logo exactly as supplied. Modified
distributions must remove the official logo and use a product and package name that is clearly
different from FoxLift.

Without written permission, do not:

- use FoxLift or a confusingly similar name for a product, company, service, package, domain, or
  social account;
- use or alter the official logo for a fork or another product;
- imply that Timo Bejan sponsors, approves, or maintains your work; or
- use the marks on merchandise.

The official logo may be used unchanged to link to [foxlift.dev](https://foxlift.dev). Any other
use requires written permission from [licensing@foxlift.dev](mailto:licensing@foxlift.dev).
